## Status

Released

## Release Date

## Purpose

FLEXSTRUCT V1 establishes a complete framework for qualifying, structuring, financing and evaluating industrial flexibility opportunities.

The framework connects:

- industrial needs
- flexibility assets
- services
- business models
- contracts
- risk allocation
- finance
- investment decisions

into a single methodology.
